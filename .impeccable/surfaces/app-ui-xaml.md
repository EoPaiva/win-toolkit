Mode: Operate. Native WPF desktop app, fixed 1280x800 window. Direction pinned by the owner: the visual world of https://upaiva.dev (repository EoPaiva/terminal-cv). No concept tournament.

THESIS: the owner's personal site, operating a Windows PC. Everything on screen is real; the only decoration is the planet, and even it tells the truth when something runs.

OWN-WORLD: true black #000, panels #070707 and #0c0c0c, hairlines #1c1c1c and #2a2a2a, ink #fff, soft #d4d4d4, muted #8c8c8c, dim #5c5c5c. One signal red #ff6b6b only for failure and blocking notices. Inter Display Light for titles, Inter Display Thin for section numbers, Inter Medium tracked uppercase for labels and navigation, Inter Regular for text, Cascadia Mono for machine output. Tight negative tracking on titles, open tracking on labels. White pill with arrow for the primary action, outline pills for the rest. Thin section numbers and tracked labels above titles are the site's own signature, so they are the owner's brand here and not generic eyebrows. Monochrome particle planet with rings and comets. Terminal window with three dots and a mono title, as in the site's Ctrl K palette. No gradients on surfaces, no neon, no glass, no sidebar, no cards around lists, no progress rings.

STORY: pick what enters, watch it happen, leave with the truth. Install, drivers, scan, optimize, tools and terminal share one header: number, label, two-line title with the second line gray.

FIRST VIEWPORT: wordmark, centered tracked navigation, Ctrl K pill. Number 01, "Escolha o que entra. / Tudo na versão mais nova.", planet at right, search and profile pills, program list on hairlines, telemetry and white pill at the bottom.

FORM: top navigation; hairline rows instead of cards; the inner orbit lights in proportion to real progress and goes dark when not running; the footer hairline fills with the same value; running state shows a queue with status and time per item beside a live terminal; Ctrl K overlays a terminal that filters programs, profiles and sections.

Product truth to keep: see PRODUCT.md. Nothing decorative may read as status. Simulation must never read as applied.
